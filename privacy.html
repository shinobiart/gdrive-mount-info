Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

High Speed Clone Google Drive is a personal, single-user desktop integration running via Rclone on the owner's local Windows computer.

1. Information Accessed

The application requests access to Google Drive solely to read, write, list, and synchronize files directly between the user's local computer and their own Google Drive account.

2. Data Storage and Sharing

All OAuth credentials, tokens, and temporary VFS cache files are stored strictly locally on the user's personal machine. This application does not operate any external backend server and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or Google Drive content with third parties.

3. Revoking Access

The user may revoke access at any time through their Google Account security settings or by deleting the local Rclone configuration file.
